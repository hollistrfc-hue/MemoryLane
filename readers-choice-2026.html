1st Place – Antique/Thrift/Vintage Store

2026 Stillwater News Press Readers' Choice Awards

Today we are feeling overwhelmed—in the best way. Memory Lane Vintage Mall has been voted 1st Place in the 2026 Stillwater News Press Readers' Choice Awards for Antique/Thrift/Vintage Store, and our hearts are full.

We chose not to campaign for votes this year. No reminders, no posts, no “please vote for us.” We wanted the community to decide on its own. And you did. You chose us.

When we opened Memory Lane, our dream was simple: create a place where people feel welcome, a place with good vibes and good people, a place you want to come back to again and again.

This award tells us that dream is alive and thriving—because of YOU.

To our customers, our community, our family and our incredible vendors: thank you for believing in us, supporting us, and giving us the honor of being your #1 choice. We couldn’t do any of this without you.
