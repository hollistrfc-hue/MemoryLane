Who We Are

Memory Lane Vintage Mall ("we," "us," or "our") operates this website. We are a vintage and antique mall located at 208 W 10th Ave, Stillwater, OK 74074. You can reach us at (405) 338-7558.

Information We Collect

When you fill out a form on our site, we collect the information you submit — which may include your name, email address, phone number, and message. We collect this information only when you voluntarily provide it.

We also use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors use our site. Google Analytics collects data such as pages visited, time on site, and general location (city/region level). This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally. For more information on how Google uses data from sites that use Google Analytics, visit Google's partner sites policy.

How We Use Your Information

Information you submit through our contact or inquiry forms is used to respond to your request and communicate with you about our services. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Form Processing

AI Tools

AI tools were used to assist in building and editing this website. These tools were used during development and do not operate on this site to collect your information.

Do Not Track
